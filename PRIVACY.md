# Privacy Policy — Double Bookmarks Bar

_Last updated: 12 September 2026_

## Short version

The extension collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere and makes no network
requests at all.

## What the extension accesses, and why

**Your bookmarks.** The extension is a bookmarks bar, so it reads your bookmark
tree in order to draw it, and changes it when you rename, edit, delete, add or
drag a bookmark yourself. This happens entirely inside your browser, through
Chrome's own bookmarks API. Your bookmarks are never copied, uploaded or
transmitted.

**Your settings.** Number of rows, theme, sizes, the list of sites where the bar
is hidden, and the clipboard used by the bar's own cut/copy/paste commands are
kept in Chrome's `storage.sync`. That data lives in your browser and, if you have
Chrome sync switched on, in your own Google account. The author of the extension
has no access to it.

**Site icons.** Icons next to your bookmarks come from the favicon cache your
browser already keeps locally. Nothing is downloaded for them.

**Access to web pages.** The extension requests access to all sites because the
bookmarks bar has to be visible on every page you open, exactly like Chrome's
built-in bar. It does not read, collect or transmit the content of pages you
visit, your browsing history, form data, credentials, or anything else on a page.
Technically it adds a single element of its own inside a closed shadow root and
offsets the page by the height of the bar.

## What the extension does not do

- No data is collected, stored on a server, or sent to anyone.
- No network requests of any kind are made.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or third-party code.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
- Your default search engine, home page and other browser settings are not
  altered. The new tab page is replaced with an intentionally empty page that
  shows only the bookmarks bar; disabling the extension restores Chrome's own.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
regardless of age.
